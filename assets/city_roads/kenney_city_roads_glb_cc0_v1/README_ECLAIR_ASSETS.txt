City Roads GLB Pack - 72 Free CC0 3D Models

This is a GLB-ready convenience package based on City Kit Roads / Kenney.

Included:
- 72 GLB files in models_glb/
- Matching PNG reference renders in reference_png/ when available
- MODEL_LIST.csv
- Original Kenney license text
- Source and license verification notes

Intended use:
city layouts, street scenes, manga background blocking, modern town maps, game prototypes, and Clip Studio Paint perspective reference.
