- Source: Kenney Car Kit, mirrored at https://github.com/shorepine/kenney/tree/main/3d/car
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal. Original pack page: https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit
- Published model sizes: approximately 1.0 MB combined for these five GLBs.

- License: CC0 public domain, as stated on each model's source page.
- Sources:
  - https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/police-officer-standing-3d-person-emergency-services-9fd93114
